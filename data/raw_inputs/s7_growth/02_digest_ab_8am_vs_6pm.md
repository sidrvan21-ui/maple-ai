# Digest A/B — 07:30 vs 18:00 daily

Author: Mina  
Window: 3 weeks, 4 buildings, not Cooper (Helen refused to experiment on her people)

## Setup

Half of new opt-ins daily at 07:30, half at 18:00. Weekly still Sunday 18:00 for all.

## What we saw (scribbled)

Morning group: more “this saved my commute” anecdotes (Priya-like).  
Evening group: fewer deletes in week 1, more “I already lived the day” shrugs.

We did not have significance. Ravi said “ship morning it’s the brand.” Mina said “then it wasn’t an experiment.”

**Decision actually made:** default 07:30 for role=renter or unknown; 18:00 if they tapped owner at signup. Role still often wrong.
