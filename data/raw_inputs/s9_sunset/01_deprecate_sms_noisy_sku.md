# Proposal — kill SMS “everything” and the noisy Instant SKU

Author: Jules  
Date: 2027-11-03

In late 2027 we offered **Porter Ping SMS** as an add-on after two councils demanded it for “seniors without smartphones.” It became: blast every Instant to SMS. Cost spiked. One building used it for cats. Recipients used “STOP” and also called Helen.

This is the decline of a **feature**, not of Porter Notice.

Recommend:

- NSC: internal, Nov  
- End of sale: no new SMS add-on  
- End of expansion: existing SMS buildings cannot add types  
- End of SMS: 90 days, then push+email only  
- Keep Porter Notice  

Ravi will say SMS is inclusion. Offer **one** weekly SMS digest for opted seniors, not Instant-to-SMS. Different product, maybe later. Not this SKU.
