## How he really communicates

Template emails. After-hours: an on-call who texts the concierge if the building has one. Many Mount Pleasant / Main St buildings have **no desk**.

He **likes BuildingLink**. He said this six times. He wants us to hear it.

> “I already have a system of record. You are a notification layer. If you cannot sit beside BuildingLink or Flagship I will not rip anything out for a two-person startup.”

This is the opposite of Helen’s “I will not buy BuildingLink.” Same ecosystem, two buyers.

## Jobs (his)

- Proof he notified: timestamp, channel, building, who published

## He will block us if

We require residents to create accounts before the first alert. “Adoption dies. Push from a magic link the first time or I walk.”

We store visitor logs or camera stuff. “That’s a different product and a PIPA nightmare. Stay in notices.”
