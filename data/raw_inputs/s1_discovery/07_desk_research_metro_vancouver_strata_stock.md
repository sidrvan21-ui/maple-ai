# Desk research — Metro Vancouver strata / condo stock

Author: Samir  
Date: 2026-03-08 (before most interviews — some numbers are stale vs later notes)

Sources mixed: CMHC starts, City of Vancouver open data, a 2024 CHOA blog, one Colliers multifamily note someone forwarded. **Not a TAM model.** I will not own a board slide from this file alone.

## What I think I know

- City of Vancouver has a very high share of households in apartments vs Canadian average. “Most people here live in a building with a council” is directionally true downtown / Yaletown / Olympic Village / Coal Harbour / Mount Pleasant mid-rises.
- Metro includes Burnaby, Richmond, Surrey, North Van, etc. Surrey high-rise growth is real; **we did not interview Surrey.**
- Purpose-built rental ≠ strata. Porter v1 story is **strata notices** (council + PM). Rental-only towers are a different buyer (one landlord). Do not mix door counts.

## Building size bands (guess, for beachhead)

Jules asked for 80–400 unit towers. That excludes:

- 12-unit walk-ups on Main (Helen’s cousin; they use a paper board)
- 600+ door downtown monsters (different politics, often already on BuildingLink)

Sweet spot anecdotally: 120–250 doors, one PM, weak desk or no desk.
