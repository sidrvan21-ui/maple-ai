# Interview — Priya Sharma (renter)

- Date: 2026-03-12
- Building: The Cooper, 188 Drake, Yaletown (~180 units) — she is not sure of the exact count, “maybe 160?”
- Interviewer: Jules (research)
- Length: 41 min, lobby Starbucks then walked to David Lam park
- Consent: verbal, first name + neighbourhood only in any external writeup

## What she actually does today

Gets building news from a WhatsApp group the last tenant admin-added her to. 214 unread. She muted it in January after a 40-message thread about a missing Amazon box that was not hers.

Strata also emails PDFs. She files them in a Gmail label she never opens. Last water shutoff: she found out when the tap coughed air at 7:10 a.m. while making coffee for a 8 a.m. standup. The PDF had gone out the night before at 11:40 p.m. Subject line: “RE: RE: Notice — Domestic Cold Water — Riser 3.”

## Jobs she described (not our words)

“I need to know if tomorrow morning is going to be ruined before I go to sleep. Not after.”

Fire alarm test last month — she was on a client call. The WhatsApp ping came 4 minutes after the bells. She wants a **push the night before** and a **same-morning reminder**, not a novel in chat.

She does **not** want amenity booking in v1. “I book the gym on a paper sheet downstairs. That’s fine. Just don’t let me miss the elevator being down on moving day.”

## Cadence she asked for (unprompted)

- Instant: elevator, water, fire, access, safety
- Daily: “what is happening in my building today” — 7:30 a.m. local, she said twice
- Weekly: “Sunday night, what’s coming” — move-ins, window washing, parkade sweeping
- Monthly: she shrugged. “Maybe fees and like… is the boiler dying.” Not her job as a renter. Owner would care more.
