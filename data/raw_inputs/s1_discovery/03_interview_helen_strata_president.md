# Interview — Helen Okonkwo (strata council president)

- Date: 2026-03-21
- Building: The Cooper, Yaletown — **184 residential + 6 CRUs**. She has the strata plan. (Priya said ~160. Derek’s file says 180. Helen is the source of record for this building.)
- Interviewer: Samir (research). Jules could not make it.
- Length: 62 min, council room in the amenity

## Her actual workflow

She drafts notices in Word, emails the property manager (Derek’s firm, Cascade Residential — different contact than Derek’s own interview, see his file). Cascade “formats” and sends. Turnaround: 6 hours if someone is at a keyboard, 2 days if it is a Friday.

She has been burned: a contractor showed up for riser work a day early. WhatsApp exploded. She is the face. She does not want another portal she has to learn.

## What she will buy

**Per-building flat fee.** She said “per door” three times like a slur.

> “I am not paying you $3 times 184 so you can email my residents something I already wrote. Quote me the building. If you cannot, I will keep Word.”

Samir noted: this contradicts Marcus (who thinks per-door from operating fund is normal) and our pricing analog memo in s2 (which assumes $3.50/door). **Do not hide this.**

## Cadence she will publish

- Instant: she will type, someone must approve if it is a bylaw threat (legal). Unclear who. Open question.
- Daily digest: she does **not** want to write a daily. “Auto-compile what we already posted. I am not a newspaper.”
- Weekly: she would spend 10 minutes Friday.
- Monthly: she wants the treasurer’s numbers in it. Treasurer is 74 and uses a fax-to-email bridge. Risk.
