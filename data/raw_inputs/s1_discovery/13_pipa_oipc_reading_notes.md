## What I think matters for Porter

- **Strata is the organization** under PIPA for a lot of resident personal information. They need a privacy officer (often the PM or a council member who does not know they are).
- **Porter would be a service provider.** Contracts must say we keep it secure, minimize, delete, and don’t use resident lists to market gyms.
- Collect only what a notice needs: building, severity, time window, channel. **Not** “unit 1204’s toilet.”
- Renters vs owners: we will have emails and phone numbers. Magic-link login (Derek) still creates an account. Retention policy needed before launch, not after.
- OIPC has **strata-specific** guidance. Someone must read the actual PDF before S5. I skimmed. I did not file a checklist yet. (That lives later if we get that far.)

## Open questions for counsel (do not answer in a PRD without a lawyer)

1. Is a push about “water off on floors 12–18” personal information? (probably not)  
2. Is a list of opted-in phone numbers the strata’s or ours?  
3. Cross-border hosting (US LLM / US push vendor) — flag for BC residents.  
4. Can a council member see who opened an alert? That’s surveillance-adjacent. Default **no**.
