# Interview — Marcus Chen (owner-occupier)

- Date: 2026-03-18
- Building: Village Lofts, Athletes Way, Olympic Village — 156 units (he checked the Form B)
- Interviewer: Jules
- Length: 55 min, his unit, then walked the seawall
- Note: he is also on the **finance committee**, not council

## How information fails

AGM package was 91 pages in a Dropbox link emailed 11 days before the meeting (Strata Property Act timing — he knows the 14-day rule and was annoyed they cut it close). Special levy vote for park railings: $2,400 his share. He found out a neighbour had already run the numbers in a spreadsheet that never left a Signal chat of 9 people.

Monthly: he wants **one page** — opening balance vibe, upcoming levy risk, insurance renewal month, “are we about to get a depreciation report surprise.” Not a PDF of the entire general ledger.

## Jobs

Functional: see money + disruption on one timeline.  
Emotional: not feel like the “clued-in clique” is 8 owners who drink with the president.  
Social: he would share a clean monthly to the WeChat group (half the floor is Mandarin-first). App must not be English-only forever — flag for later, not v1.

## Cadence

- Instant: anything that affects access or safety
- Daily: he would **off** daily. “I’m not a concierge. Daily is for renters who miss shutoffs.”
- Weekly: yes — Sunday
- Monthly: **this is the product for owners**, in his view. He ranked monthly > weekly > daily.

Conflicts with Priya (daily is the product). Product will have to segment. Jules wrote “PERSONA SPLIT??” in the margin and did not resolve it.

## Competitors

Uses BuildingLink at his **sister’s** building in North Van. “It’s a portal. I log in when I have a package. I do not open it for news. News is still email.”

Flagship / Condo Control: “the PM loves it. Owners get a login they forget.”

## WTP

Would approve **$2.50–$4.00 per door per month** from the **operating fund** if it replaced the PM’s “we sent an email” excuse. Will vote no if it is $8+ or bundled into a 12-module suite they will not use.

## Quote

> “Don’t sell me amenity booking. Sell me that I will never be surprised by a $2,400 levy because someone buried it in appendix C.”
