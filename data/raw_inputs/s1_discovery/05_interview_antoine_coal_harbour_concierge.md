# Interview — Antoine Leclerc (front desk / concierge)

- Date: 2026-03-28
- Building: The Shoreline, Coal Harbour — 240 units, 24h desk, unionized contractor (not Cascade staff)
- Interviewer: Jules
- Length: 33 min, on a smoke-break bench, then 10 min more when a resident interrupted

## His day

He is the notification system. People stop at the desk. He writes a paper log. He also posts on a lobby iPad that runs a generic “announcements” web page the PM updates twice a week.

Packages: he will not let us touch package flow. “That’s my job and half my tips conversation.” Out of wedge. Confirm with scoping.

## What breaks

Elevator techs tell **him**, not the PM, 20 minutes before they lock out a cab. He texts a group of 8 “regulars.” Everyone else finds out at the door with a suitcase.

Fire alarm testing: posted in the elevator two days prior **on paper**. Renters who use the parkade elevator miss it.

## Cadence from the desk

He would publish:

- Instant: if we give him a **big button** on a phone, not a laptop CMS
- Daily: auto. He will not write prose at 6 a.m.
- Weekly: “maybe”
- Monthly: not his job

## Quote

> “If your app needs me to sit down and ‘compose a digest,’ I will not. I have a line. One tap: elevator 2 down, 40 minutes. That’s the product.”

## Skepticism

Tried a building Instagram in 2023. Died in 6 weeks. “Residents don’t follow the building. They follow restaurants.”

Asked if we are “another American company that doesn’t know a strata from an HOA.” Jules said we are Vancouver-first. He said “then say strata, say Form B, say depreciation report, or I know you’re faking it.”
