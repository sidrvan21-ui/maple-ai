# Seasonality — AGMs, budgets, when they will actually buy

Author: Jules  
Date: 2026-04-19  
Sources: Helen, Marcus (finance committee), Derek, CHOA calendar vibes

## The year as they live it

- **Jan–Mar:** depreciation report hangover, insurance quotes, leak season. Notices spike. Councils tired. Bad time to pitch “another app” unless we ride a failed shutoff.  
- **Apr–Jun:** **AGM season** for a lot of Vancouver stratas. Packages, elections, special levies. Marcus-type buyers are paying attention. Helen-type presidents are drowning. Pitch **after** the AGM or as “we will make next year’s package less cursed.”  
- **Jul–Aug:** people gone. Quorum hell. Do not expect a vote.  
- **Sep–Nov:** budget for next fiscal. **Best window for a line item** if we are cheap and named.  
- **Dec:** dead.

## Implication for Porter GTM

Launch narrative should not be “we go live Canada Day.” Prefer **September budget insert** or **right after a painful spring AGM**.

Derek: “I will not onboard 8 buildings in May.” Believe him.

## Product implication

Monthly digest in April–June should have an “AGM / levy” slot or we look clueless. That is a content template, not a new module.
